Peace Corps

What is the Peace Corps?

The Peace Corps is a United States government agency that sends American Volunteers to work alongside communities around the world. Volunteers collaborate with local partners on community-identified priorities while building relationships and promoting cultural exchange.

Peace Corps Colombia

Colombia was one of the original Peace Corps countries, with the first group of Volunteers arriving in 1961. The program was suspended in the early 1980s and returned to Colombia in 2010. Since its return, Volunteers have worked alongside Colombian communities in areas including English education, youth development, and community economic development.

Community Economic Development (CED)

Community Economic Development, or CED, is one of the Peace Corps programs in Colombia. The program supports the growth of local and regional economies through entrepreneurship, business advising, livelihood skills, and personal money management.

What CED Volunteers Do

CED Volunteers work alongside local teachers, government officials, entrepreneurs, business owners, women, and youth. Their work can include entrepreneurship education, basic business skills, financial education, community courses, and other locally prioritized activities.
