The Star Model

The Star Model is a simple tool that helps entrepreneurs discover and evaluate business ideas. It encourages you to consider five important factors: your passions, your capabilities, the opportunities available, your environment, and your competition.

The Five Points of the Star

01

Passion

What do you enjoy doing? Think about your interests, hobbies, and activities that motivate you.

Example: Homemade Yogurt

You enjoy cooking, experimenting with flavors, and preparing homemade yogurt.

02

Capabilities

What are you good at? Identify your skills, knowledge, experience, and resources.

Example: Homemade Yogurt

You know how to make yogurt, handle ingredients safely, and communicate with customers.

03

Possibilities

What opportunities exist? Look for problems you could solve or needs that are not being fully met.

Example: Homemade Yogurt

Students and families in your neighborhood are looking for affordable, fresh snacks.

04

Environment

What is happening around you? Consider your community, local economy, customer habits, and available resources.

Example: Homemade Yogurt

Local farmers sell milk and fruit, and neighborhood shops could help distribute your yogurt.

05

Competition

Who else offers something similar? Identify businesses selling similar products and compare their prices, quality, and customer service.

Example: Homemade Yogurt

Nearby stores sell commercial yogurt, but you could offer fresh, locally made yogurt with unique fruit flavors.

How to Use the Star Model

Start by writing your business idea in the center of the star. Then examine each of the five points and answer its guiding question.

  1. Define your business idea.
  2. Answer the questions for each of the five categories.
  3. Write down examples, observations, and available resources.
  4. Identify your strengths, opportunities, and potential challenges.
  5. Decide what you need to research, learn, or improve before starting.

What Should You Learn?

A good business idea requires more than passion alone. You also need the skills to develop it, customers willing to buy it, favorable conditions, and a way to compete.
