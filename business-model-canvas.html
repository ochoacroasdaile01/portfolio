Business Model Canvas

The Business Model Canvas helps you organize your business idea on one page. It explains what you sell, who your customers are, how your business operates, and how it earns money. Explore its nine sections using a homemade yogurt business as an example.

01

Customer Segments

The groups of people you want to sell to. Who needs your product and can afford it?

Example: Homemade Yogurt

Students looking for a snack and families in your neighborhood.

02

Value Proposition

The reason customers would choose your product. What need does it meet or benefit does it offer?

Example: Homemade Yogurt

Fresh yogurt made with fruit and local milk at an affordable price.

03

Channels

How customers discover, buy, and receive your product.

Example: Homemade Yogurt

WhatsApp posts, local fairs, and deliveries.

04

Customer Relationships

How you treat customers and encourage them to return.

Example: Homemade Yogurt

Friendly service, reliable orders, and asking for feedback on flavors.

05

Revenue Streams

The ways your business brings in money. Revenue is not profit: you still need to subtract costs.

Example: Homemade Yogurt

Selling individual yogurt cups and family-sized containers.

06

Key Resources

The equipment, materials, money, and knowledge your business needs.

Example: Homemade Yogurt

A refrigerator, ingredients, containers, and knowledge of safe food preparation.

07

Key Activities

The most important tasks you must perform to run your business.

Example: Homemade Yogurt

Buying ingredients, making and refrigerating yogurt, promoting it, and delivering orders.

08

Key Partners

The people or businesses you work with to keep your business running.

Example: Homemade Yogurt

Local milk suppliers, packaging suppliers, and stores that sell your yogurt.

09

Cost Structure

Everything you spend to operate your business.

Example: Homemade Yogurt

Milk, fruit, containers, electricity, transportation, and payment for labor.
